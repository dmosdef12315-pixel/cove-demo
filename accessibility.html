Accessibility
& assistance.

We want planning a visit to The Cove to be straightforward and comfortable for everyone.

Using this website

The website is designed to support keyboard navigation, screen readers, text resizing, and reduced-motion preferences. You can skip directly to the main content using the first link on each page.

The full HTML menu includes dish descriptions and prices. It is the preferred option for screen readers, smaller screens, and enlarged text. A copy of the restaurant’s original PDF is also available, but that legacy document may not offer the same accessibility features.

Need help or found a barrier?

Please call (401) 364-9222 or email marisa@thecoveri.com. If you are reporting a website issue, tell us which page you were using and what got in your way.

Planning your visit

For questions about the restaurant’s physical access, seating, food allergies, or dietary needs, please call before your visit so the team can discuss your needs directly.

External services

Directions open in Google Maps, and social links open Facebook. Those services have their own accessibility features and policies. You can always contact the restaurant directly for assistance.
