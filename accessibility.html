Website accessibility

This website is designed with accessibility in mind, using WCAG 2.2 Level AA as its target.

Using this website

You can navigate the site using a keyboard, enlarge text with your browser, and read the full food menu as HTML. Pages use clear headings, descriptive links, and visible keyboard focus. The site does not use automatic slideshows or require motion.

The food menu

The HTML food menu is the primary accessible version. The original restaurant PDF is also available as a separate reference and may not offer the same accessibility features.

Need assistance?

If you have difficulty using a page, or need information in another format, contact The Cove Restaurant at (401) 364-9222 or marisa@thecoveri.com. If possible, include the page and the issue you experienced.

External services

Links to Facebook and Google Maps lead to third-party services with their own accessibility features. You can also contact the restaurant directly for information or directions.
