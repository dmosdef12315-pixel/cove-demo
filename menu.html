A few things to know

  • Sharing a dinner carries a $3 charge.
  • Parties of seven or more have a 20% gratuity added.
  • Visa, Mastercard and American Express are accepted, with a $15 minimum.
  • Tell your server about any food allergies in your party.

Food safety advisory (**): Eating raw or undercooked meat, poultry, seafood, shellfish, or eggs can raise the risk of foodborne illness, particularly for people with certain medical conditions. Thorough cooking reduces that risk.
